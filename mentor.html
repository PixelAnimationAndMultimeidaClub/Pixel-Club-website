// FACULTY ADVISOR

Dr. Wong Yi Chieng

3D ANIMATION MAYA

12+ years experience in character rigging, production pipeline architecture, and technical direction for feature-length 3D animation.

// BACKGROUND STORY

Dr. Wong’s journey began in classic 3D animation studios, where he worked long nights troubleshooting broken character rigs and resolving broken rendering pipelines for feature films. After earning his doctorate in Computer Graphics and Digital Production, he transitioned to academia with a simple mission: bridging the gap between artistic intuition and technical discipline.

Known around the lab as the "Pipeline Whisperer," Dr. Wong believes that clean topology and efficient rigging are what give digital characters their soul. When he’s not guiding students through complex Maya node graphs or Python automation scripts, he can usually be found analyzing the subtle physics of real-world movement to help animators make their weight distribution look believable.

"Structure doesn't restrict creativity—it gives it a solid skeleton to dance on."
// GUEST INDUSTRY MENTOR

Chan Yan Ling

MOTION GRAPHICS AFTER EFFECTS

Senior Motion Designer specializing in commercial title sequences, keyframe animations, UI interactions, and visual effects pipelines.

// BACKGROUND STORY

Yan Ling spent the early years of her career in fast-paced creative agencies across Southeast Asia, crafting keyframe visual sequences for international brands, interactive UI transitions, and high-energy commercial title cards. Her breakthrough came when a kinetic typography project she directed went viral across design showcase platforms, opening doors to direct major visual campaigns.

As a guest mentor, Yan Ling brings raw real-world energy into the studio. She teaches students how to make movement feel punchy, dynamic, and purposeful without overloading the screen with unnecessary effects. She is particularly passionate about micro-interactions, color theory in motion, and helping students build pitch-ready demo reels that catch the eyes of creative directors.

"If a frame doesn't serve the story or the emotion, it's just visual noise."
// TECHNICAL DIRECTOR

Kong Jun Yang

VIDEO EDITING COLOR GRADING

Specialist in DaVinci Resolve color workflows, post-production audio mastering, and broadcast editorial management.

// BACKGROUND STORY

Jun Yang’s passion for visual media started behind the camera, but his true craft was forged in the dark room of the editing suite. With years of experience managing post-production pipelines for broadcast media, documentaries, and indie short films, he understands how much a story is re-written on the timeline.

Specializing in DaVinci Resolve color architecture, surround-sound mixing, and post-production editorial workflows, Jun Yang treats color grading like fine art painting. He can spend hours dialing in the shadow tones of a single scene to evoke just the right mood. Students turn to him when their project is shot and animated, but needs that final polish to transform it into a cinematic experience.

"Editing is the invisible rhythm of cinema; if the audience notices the cuts, you haven't finished the job."
// CONCEPT ARTIST

Hong Chee Ren

2D ANIMATION STORYBOARDING

Focuses on visual storytelling, character concept designs, cinematic keyframes, and traditional frame-by-frame animation techniques.

// BACKGROUND STORY

Chee Ren grew up with sketchbooks tucked under his arm, fascinated by traditional animation, wuxia visual aesthetics, and world-building through environmental concept art. After years of producing keyframes, character sheets, and storyboard animatics for indie games and animated shorts, he joined PIXEL to mentor the next generation of visual storytellers.

Whether he’s breaking down frame-by-frame timing or teaching the fundamentals of silhouette design and lighting composition, Chee Ren emphasizes narrative intent above technical perfection. He encourages students to look at the world around them—nature, architecture, classical art, and human expressions—to give their digital art depth and personal expression.

"Before you render a single detail, make sure your silhouette tells a clear story."
